Back up or move your progress

Use this to move your progress to another device or site, or to keep a backup. Copy my progress copies everything (levels, review schedule, weak spots, answer history) as text. Paste that text into this section on the other copy of the course and press Import. Importing merges: for every item, the more advanced record is kept, so nothing is lost.
